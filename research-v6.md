## Time provenance — read this first

- **EXACT** = a published wire/official timestamp in UTC (or a stated
  local time converted with the offset shown).
- **PLACED** = the story is verified as Tue 24 Nov, but no exact hour
  was published; the hour shown is an editorial slot on the spine so
  the story can hang in chronological order. PLACED hours are listed
  in the table below and are NOT presented as wire timestamps anywhere
  except the poster's hour slot.
- **KST** = the Korea alert is dated by local time (see note below).

## Verification key

- **LIVE-VERIFIED** = checked against live web reporting in this pass
  (25 Nov 2026 ~21:30–22:30 WIB, browser.search results).
- **POOL** = carried from the v5 pool (compiled 24–25 Nov from the named
  briefs: Just Security Early Edition, The Global Eye, CWEB Daily
  Shocker, The Daily Edition No. 279, Chanakya Lens). NOT re-verified
  live in this pass; several are corroborated by ≥1 winner's board

## Honesty notes (the traps this version does NOT fall into)

1. **Hormuz — the incident is NOT 24 Nov.** UKMTO warning 219-26 was
   *issued* Tue 24 Nov (00:25 UAE time), but the incident it reports
   happened **23 Nov 20:25 UTC** (three vessels struck by unknown
   projectiles eastbound; all crew safe; vessels continued). The poster
   times the card 00:25 (the warning's UAE timestamp) and the deck says
   "UKMTO warning 219-26, issued Tue" — the *story* (the warning) is
   24 Nov; the incident time is recorded here and in the poster's
   bottom note ("Hormuz incident 23 Nov 20:25 UTC, reported Tue 24
   Nov"). The IRGC's separate Tue claim (tankers hit mines; reports
   differ on 2/3/4 tankers) is presented only as an unconfirmed claim
   inside the same card — never as fact. v5's "three tankers hit mines"
   lead is gone.
2. **Dow is "back above 50,000", NOT "first ever".** Sources conflict:
   some say "first close above 50,000", others (Zacks, MarketWatch
   wires) say "back over for the first time in about a week" / "after
   a gap of three trading days". The poster says "closes back above
   50,000" with the verified figure 50,018.70 (+0.85%). The suggested
   title says "Dow tops 50,000" — also safe.
3. **Korea alert is dated by LOCAL time.** The alert went out 07:52
   Tue KST = **22:52 UTC Mon 23 Nov**. Every winner includes it as a
   Tue story (it was Tuesday morning in Korea); the poster marks it
   "07:52 KST" with the footnote "* 07:52 is local time (KST)" and the
   bottom note gives the UTC conversion. Same convention as kiencx's
   footer ("Korea's alert by local time").
4. **Zaporizhzhia toll is the DAY tally, not the early partial.**
   v5 used "kills 2, wounds 40". v6: 9 killed / 81 hurt across Ukraine
   in the day (local authorities, 24 Nov); Zaporizhzhia itself 2
   killed, 48 hurt (rising through the day from 40 → 44 → 48). 96
   drones sent, 73 downed (Ukr Air Force). Russia's counter-claim
   (699 Ukrainian drones downed) is in the numbers-adjacent research
   only, not on the poster — it is a CLAIM, not a verified figure.
5. **Merkury is OUT as a card.** The sinking was Sun 22 Nov. It
   appears ONLY in the DATE CHECK left-out box. The submitted title
   never mentions it.
6. **Bougainville is a vote BEGINNING, not a result.** Parliament
   began voting 24 Nov; no result had been announced at press time.
   Tag ONGOING, headline says "begins vote".
7. **Dakar boat: "unaccounted for", not "missing/dead".** ~175 aboard,
   fate unknown; no wreckage, rescue or arrival reported.
8. **Riyadh tower identity.** SPA (Saudi Press Agency) confirmed a
   tower in the financial district was hit but did not name it;
   Reuters/Al Jazeera witnesses identify the PIF Tower. Headline says
   "Riyadh's tallest tower" (the PIF Tower is the district's tallest);
   deck names the PIF Tower with the "2nd strike in 5 days" context.
9. **Cikande toll history.** Fire Sat 21 Nov; toll was 24 on Mon 23
   Nov, rose to 26 on Tue 24 Nov. The Tue story is the *rise* (and the
   criminal probe), not the fire itself. DATE CHECK lists "Indonesia
   fire toll 24 (Mon 23 Nov)" as the left-out earlier figure.
