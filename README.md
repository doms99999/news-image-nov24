# The World in One Day — Tuesday 24 November 2026

One image summing up the world news of Tuesday 24 November 2026, made for make.fun's news-image-20261124 bounty. **The World in One Day** is a dense broadsheet, not a list: a full-width lead story (the Strait of Hormuz tanker strikes, tagged CLAIM because US Central Command denies it), a "day in numbers" strip, then 12 more image cards and 16 numbered "ALSO ON" briefs — 29 stories in all, every one dated with an exact UTC time and a specific place, each carrying a certainty tag (CONFIRMED / OFFICIAL / REPORTED / CLAIM) with a legend. The photos are real copyright-free images (Wikimedia Commons, credited under each card; the Ebola card is an illustration because no free photo of the Ituri response exists), and a real world map ("Where it happened", drawn from Natural Earth land data) carries all 29 numbered pins at bottom-right. The masthead carries the official make.fun lockup beside the title. The collage panels:

- **Tankers burn in Hormuz** — Iran refuses talks until its conditions are met as three oil tankers hit mines in the Strait of Hormuz and the US naval blockade holds; Vance and Rubio host Qatar's prime minister at the White House.
- **Markets: TAIEX +8.99%** — Asia's chip stocks surge while Wall Street cools (Nasdaq −1.48%, Nvidia −5.86%) with the SEC accounting probe still open; the Nasdaq leap the pool first dated to the 24th actually happened on Monday the 23rd and was corrected here.
- **NATO warns on space weapon** — Mark Rutte says the alliance will defend against threats in space after reports Russia is preparing a nuclear-armed satellite.
- **Gaza strikes kill seven** — Israeli airstrikes kill Hamas's internal security director Naeem Abu Nahel and six others, including police officers and a UNRWA worker.

The masthead names the day, as the brief asks, and carries the official make.fun lockup from the press kit beside the title. Three date errors from the earlier pool were caught by the hard date check and fixed in this version: the COP31 collapse was Sunday 22 November (dropped), the Nasdaq +6.51% leap was Monday 23 November (reframed around the real 24 November story, TAIEX +8.99%), and the Merkury sinking was 22 November (reframed on the genuine 24 November developments).
